VISIBLE ISSUE
During an active PDF upload, the progress panel exists and updates, but its content sits too low inside the surrounding rounded container. The status text/percentage can be seen near the lower edge while the progress track and/or other content is partially hidden.

The desired result is:
- the progress panel remains INSIDE the protected-PDF/product container,
- it is horizontally centered with a small inset from both sides,
- it is raised slightly compared with its current position,
- its internal status text, percentage and progress track are vertically centered and fully visible,
- no ancestor container clips it.

CONFIRMED CSS FINDINGS
The source CSS contains:

.vava-selections-admin .vava-selections-product-item{
  overflow:hidden;
}

The progress component itself is a CSS grid and the protected PDF field is nested inside that product item. Even after changing the PDF field height to auto, the ancestor overflow rule can still clip visible progress content.

The shared progress styles also do not force max-content grid rows/alignment, so if extra vertical space is inherited the progress rows can sit too low instead of staying compact and centered.
